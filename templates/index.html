    Why Choose Crop Advisor?

    Clear crop guidance for better farm decisions. It shows when conditions suit a crop, flags uncertainty, and does not guess when the inputs are unusual.

    • A practical shortlist of crops to consider
    • Soil, weather, rainfall, and groundwater information in one report
    • Simple help for fertilizer, irrigation, profit, and crop rotation planning
    • Clear warnings when a result needs extra care
    • Built to support discussions with local agriculture experts

    About Crop Advisor

    Crop Advisor helps farmers and agricultural teams compare crop options using soil-test values and local weather information.

    The recommendation model uses soil nutrients, soil pH, and weather. Your location helps provide local weather, groundwater, and support information. Fertilizer doses, irrigation plans, prices, yields, and scheme eligibility are estimates; confirm them with your KVK, Agriculture Department, or agricultural university before spending money.
