    Why choose Crop Advisor?

    It tells you when it is unsure, and refuses to guess when your input is outside what it has seen.

    • ML-powered recommendations, 8 models compared with cross-validation
    • Top-3 crop comparison with probabilities
    • Prediction confidence and stability checks
    • Out-of-range detection that withholds unreliable answers
    • Planning tools for fertilizer, profit and rotation

    About Crop Advisor

    Crop Advisor is an academic project that turns the open Crop Recommendation dataset into a practical decision-support website for farmers, extension officers and students.

    Model predictions use only soil nutrients, pH and weather. State is used for market prices. Reference prices, doses and durations are indicative and must be confirmed locally.
