    Why Choose Crop Advisor?

    It transparently informs you when confidence is low, and refuses to guess when inputs fall outside historical boundaries.

    • ML-powered recommendations, 8 algorithms compared via 5-fold CV
    • Top-3 crop probabilistic ranking with entropy measurement
    • Noise-injection stability testing on every prediction
    • Mahalanobis distance out-of-range anomaly detection
    • Integrated planning for fertilizers, profit, and seasonal rotation

    About Crop Advisor

    Crop Advisor is an applied machine learning project that converts research datasets into a practical decision-support system for farmers, agricultural officers, and researchers.

    Model predictions use strictly soil nutrients, pH, and weather. Location (State) is used for mandi market prices only. Doses, prices, and yields are indicative and should be confirmed with your local KVK or agricultural university.
